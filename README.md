## 💡 How I Think About Product

### 01 — Start with the problem

I focus on the underlying business problem rather than immediately jumping to a feature or technology.

### 02 — Make the workflow visible

Complex workflows often hide the biggest opportunities for automation. I map the process, identify friction, and quantify the opportunity.

### 03 — Use AI where it creates leverage

AI should eliminate meaningful operational effort, improve decision quality, or unlock capabilities that weren't previously practical.

### 04 — Keep humans where judgment matters

For regulated and high-risk workflows, I design systems where automation and human judgment work together rather than treating AI as a black box.

### 05 — Measure outcomes

A product isn't successful because it shipped. I measure success through business outcomes: time saved, cost reduced, quality improved, revenue created, or risk reduced.
