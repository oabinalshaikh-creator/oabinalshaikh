How I Think About Product

Identify the friction. Understand the data. Ship. Measure.

01

Start with the problem

I focus on the underlying business problem rather than immediately jumping to a feature or a technology. The solution space stays open until the problem is actually understood.

02

Make the workflow visible

Complex workflows hide the biggest opportunities for automation. I map the process, identify the friction, and quantify the opportunity before anyone writes code.

03

Use AI where it creates leverage

AI should eliminate meaningful operational effort, improve decision quality, or unlock capabilities that weren't previously practical — not decorate a roadmap.

04

Keep humans where judgment matters

For regulated and high-risk workflows, I design systems where automation and human judgment work together, rather than treating a model as a black box.

05

Measure outcomes

A product isn't successful because it shipped. Success is time saved, cost reduced, quality improved, revenue created, or risk reduced — and it's stated in numbers.
